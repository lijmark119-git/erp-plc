# Advanced sales and customer management

This is an additive extension of the existing customer-to-cash workflows. The existing credit controls, quotations and order approvals, delivery and returns, invoicing, payments, receivables aging, and customer/salesperson profitability reports remain available.

## CRM and customer activity

- Open **Sales → CRM & activity** to record leads or opportunities, optionally associate an existing customer, and capture stage, estimated value, expected close date, source, and notes.
- Users with customer-management permission can move records through New, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, and Lost stages. Updates are written to the sales audit trail.
- Log calls, email, meetings, notes, or follow-ups against a customer or CRM record. Follow-up dates and the activity timeline are retained.
- The CRM screen provides rule-based follow-up suggestions from due activity records. They are not generated by an AI model.
- Existing customers remain in the customer register; CRM records may be created before a customer is onboarded.

Customer profiles include invoice-backed purchase counts, lifetime billed value, last purchase date, repeat/at-risk segments, outstanding receivables, and overdue warnings. These values are calculated from the organization’s posted sales records, not from browser seed data.

## Pricing and quote revisions

- Open **Sales → Customer pricing** to configure a customer-specific price list with a currency and effective date range.
- Users without sales-order or quotation management permission must use the active customer price or the product catalogue price. Price overrides are rejected server-side.
- A quotation can be revised from its workflow row. Revisions are new draft quotations linked to the original, retain the copied lines, and go through the existing submit/approval workflow independently.

## Sales order controls

- Order creation includes a UUID idempotency key. Retrying the same request returns the original order; reusing the key with changed order data is rejected.
- Users with sales-order management permission can amend a draft order’s delivery date/payment terms and cancel draft or submitted orders. Each change requires a reason and is recorded with before/after data in the audit trail.
- Approved orders cannot be amended or cancelled through these controls; delivery, invoice, and return processes remain responsible for fulfillment and posted inventory/accounting effects.

## Delivery, returns, and performance

- Delivery confirmation can record the recipient, a proof-of-delivery reference, and the delivery timestamp. A shipment tracking URL can be saved at the shipped step.
- Delivered shipments can have a return request recorded with a reason. A user with delivery-management permission must approve or reject it with a resolution. Approval authorizes the physical return but does not change inventory. `POST /api/sales/return-requests/:requestId/inspection` records received, restockable, quarantined, and damaged quantities per delivery line; only after inspection are inventory receipts and accounting journals posted. Quarantined and damaged quantities are recorded as traced lots and are excluded from available-to-promise inventory.
- Return inspections do not automatically issue customer credit notes or refunds. Those require an authorized invoice/payment allocation workflow; no return-to-credit-note automation is currently implemented.
- Commission rules are configurable per salesperson and effective date. The performance report estimates commissions from posted net invoice sales; it does not accrue payroll or accounting liabilities.
- Monthly targets can be configured by salesperson and compared with posted net invoice sales over a selected date range.
- The next-month sales figure is a statistical estimate based on average net invoiced sales over the preceding three complete months. It is not an AI-generated prediction.

## Receivable disputes

- From **Sales → Receivables**, open a dispute against an outstanding receivable.
- Authorized users can move disputes to review, resolve them with a resolution note, or reject them with a resolution note.
- Dispute records do not change invoice balances or accounting postings; those continue through the established payment and credit workflows.

CRM records, activities, and disputes are stored in migration `032_sales_crm_disputes.sql`. Customer insights and order idempotency are added by `035_sales_customer_insights_order_controls.sql`; price lists and quotation revisions are added by `036_sales_pricing_quotation_revisions.sql`; delivery proof, return requests, commission rules, and targets are added by `037_sales_performance_delivery_returns.sql`; return inspection records are added by `044_sales_return_inspection.sql`. CRM, price list, commission, and target changes use the existing `sales:customers:manage` permission; return-request and inspection actions use `sales:delivery:manage`.
